Where the Evidence Stops

01Redirects

We tested direct requests and chains of 1 or 3 redirects in controlled HTTP fixtures.

02DNS rebinding

Four single probes cannot establish a success rate across TTL and cache conditions.

In the TTL 1, primed-cache probe, the each-hop policy stayed on the external address.
03Broader coverage

We evaluated one HTTP client stack and selected IPv4 scenarios; HTTPS and IPv6 were not measured.

Our Findings Apply to the Tested Scenarios and Environment.

Our repeated HTTP comparison covers the specific direct and redirect fixtures shown, including chains of one and three redirects. It does not establish behavior for all redirect chains. Our DNS evidence consists of four single probes through an Unbound caching resolver, not repeated rate measurements. In the each-hop probe with TTL 1 and a primed cache, the connection stayed at the external service, so the attack did not reach the internal report. This is a single observation, not a general claim about cached rebinding. The measurements use Python and PycURL/libcurl with selected IPv4 and HTTP fixtures; HTTPS, IPv6, connection reuse, other clients, and production network controls remain outside the evaluated conditions.
