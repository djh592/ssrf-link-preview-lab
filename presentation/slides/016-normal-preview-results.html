Check & Bind Preserved Normal Previews; Disabling Redirects Did Not

Four policies preserved all 150 tested normal previews. The no-redirects policy succeeded only for the 50 direct-page trials and rejected all 100 legitimate redirect trials, showing the compatibility cost of simply disabling redirects. The latency panel compares the vulnerable no-check baseline with check and bind because binding was the policy that also closed the observed DNS gap. Across zero, one, and three redirects, its median worker-side overhead was only a few milliseconds in this isolated testbed, while P95 remained close to the baseline. Latency includes only successful tasks and excludes Compose startup and image fetching. Transition with: "Combining security and utility gives a direct answer to our research question."
