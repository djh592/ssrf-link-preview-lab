What Did This Project Contribute?

01

Instrumented testbed

A working link-preview service with controlled redirect, DNS, and protected-report fixtures.

02

Comparable policies

Five defenses exercised through the same preview pipeline and scenario definitions.

03

Trial-linked evidence

Policy decisions, DNS answers, connected peers, and destination events joined by trial ID.

The testbed enables safe, controlled reproduction and like-for-like comparison of link-preview SSRF defenses.

Our design is informed by CWE-918 and the S-S-R-F prevention guidance from OWASP. Based on this guidance, our project makes three main contributions. [Click] First, we built an instrumented testbed. It contains a working link preview service, together with controlled redirect, DNS, and internal report services. This allows us to reproduce the attacks safely. [Click] Second, we implemented five defense policies in the same preview pipeline. Every policy uses the same scenarios, so we can compare them under the same conditions. [Click] Third, we record what happens during every trial. This includes the policy decision, the DNS answer, the actual connected peer, and whether the destination received the request. So we do not rely only on what appears in the preview. [Click] Together, these contributions allow us to reproduce the attacks safely and compare the defenses directly.
