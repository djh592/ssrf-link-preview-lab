We Evaluate Security and Preview Functionality Together.

These mechanisms lead to our research question: can a link-preview service block unauthorized private access without breaking useful public previews? We vary where each destination is checked and whether the checked address governs the actual connection. For security, we separately measure whether a request reaches the private service and whether its data is returned. For functionality, we measure successful previews and latency under direct URLs, redirects, and changing DNS answers. Our conclusions are scoped to the Python/libcurl testbed we evaluate.
