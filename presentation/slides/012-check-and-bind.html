How Does a Check Govern the Connection?

The Approved Address Must Be Used and Verified on Every Request Hop.

Each iteration begins with one current URL, either the submitted URL or a redirect target. The address policy resolves that target and records its decision before transport begins. Check and bind passes the selected address into libcurl through CURLOPT_RESOLVE, retaining the hostname while pinning the connection to the approved IP. The transport sends exactly one hop because automatic redirect following is disabled. It then records CURLINFO_PRIMARY_IP and rejects a peer that differs from the binding. A 3xx Location is validated and becomes the next current URL, so the policy runs again. Other experiment policies intentionally omit either later-hop checks or connection binding.
