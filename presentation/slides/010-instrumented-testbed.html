The testbed enables safe, controlled reproduction and like-for-like comparison of link-preview SSRF defenses.

Our contribution is not a new vulnerability or a new HTTP stack. It has three parts. First, we implemented an instrumented link-preview testbed with controlled redirect, DNS, and protected-report fixtures. Second, we made five policies comparable by exercising them through the same preview pipeline and scenario definitions. Third, each trial links policy decisions, DNS answers, connected peers, and independent destination-service events, so a rendered card is not our only evidence. The SSRF model and mitigation principles are informed by CWE-918 and OWASP guidance; we do not claim those mechanisms as contributions. The resulting artifact lets us reproduce the attacks in an isolated environment and compare defenses under equivalent conditions.
