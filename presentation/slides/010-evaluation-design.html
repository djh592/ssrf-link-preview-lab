We measure both security and the usefulness of normal previews.

For the evaluation, we compared five policies in controlled batch experiments. No Check performs no destination validation. Initial URL checks only the submitted URL. Each Hop checks every redirected URL. Check and Bind also binds the connection to the checked IP. No Redirects refuses to follow redirects. We ran every policy 50 times for each HTTP scenario. [Click] Normal tests covered direct pages and one or three redirects. Across 750 trials, we measured success and latency. [Click] Attack tests covered direct internal targets and redirects. Across 1,000 trials, we measured report access and disclosure. [Click] Four DNS traces linked answers, connected peers, and report events. They show the mechanism, not an attack rate.
