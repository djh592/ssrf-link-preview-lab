Did the Client Use the Address It Checked?

Per-Hop Validation Checked the External IP but Connected to the Internal IP; Binding Kept Them the Same.

These are two separate single probes under the same rebinding, TTL-zero, empty-cache condition. In the each-hop probe, validation approved the first external DNS answer. Because the transport was not bound, it resolved the hostname again, received the internal answer, and connected to the internal report. The independent report log and returned marker confirm access and disclosure. In the bound probe, the approved external IP was supplied to libcurl, the recorded peer remained the external service, and no internal access was observed. These traces establish the mechanism in this testbed; they are not repeated success-rate estimates. Transition with: "Binding closed the observed DNS gap, but a useful defense must also preserve legitimate pages and redirects."
