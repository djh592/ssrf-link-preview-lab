Why Study Link-Preview SSRF?

Fetching a user-chosen URL is the feature itself—so blocking every outbound request is not a usable defense.

Link Preview Must Fetch Public URLs—but Not Private Services.

Link preview is not the only SSRF setting, and we do not claim it is uniquely dangerous. We choose it because fetching a user-selected public page is the feature's normal behavior. A useful defense therefore cannot simply block all outbound requests without breaking the feature. It must preserve public previews while preventing access to private services. This official CVE record, submitted by Synology, shows that this boundary has failed in a real link-preview product. The record leaves the exploit vector unspecified, so do not attribute this case to redirects, DNS rebinding, or another particular mechanism. Transition with: "One difficulty is that the effective destination may change after the initial URL is checked—through HTTP redirects or DNS resolution."
